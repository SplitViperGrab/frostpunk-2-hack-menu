## 🧩 About

**Frostpunk 2 Companion** is a curated list of Frostpunk 2 open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, city planner viewer, resource tracker
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — faction catalog, law overview, overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — city layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 City Helpers
- Resource Tracker — follow coal, food, and material flow
- Heat Map Notes — visualize generator coverage
- Build Planner — sketch district layouts before committing
- Time Pause Notes — review decisions at your own pace

### 🎨 Catalog & Factions
- Faction Overview — browse factions and their agendas
- Law Catalog — organize researched laws and effects
- Custom Tag Notes — group buildings by purpose
- District Overlay — improved city visibility

### 🏋️ Strategy Layout
- Scenario Notes — segment study of each scenario
- Difficulty Overview — manually compare settings
- Budget Planner — chain production and consumption notes
